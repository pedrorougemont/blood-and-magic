Tactical Field Guide

Objective

Destroy the enemy Crypt (Map 9110) or enemy Barracks (Map 9155) while defending your Keep.

Mouse & Keyboard Controls

  • Left-Click: Select unit, building, or terrain; or confirm active command target.
  • Left-Drag: Box-select a group of friendly units.
  • Right-Click: Obvious Verb — Move selected unit(s) to tile, Attack enemy unit/building, toggle Obelisk on self, or Summon at Portal.
  • Minimap Click/Drag: Jump camera to map location.
  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Scroll camera across the 32×40 map.
  • Hotkeys: M Move, A Attack, G Guard/Obelisk, S Skill/Transfer, T Transform/Summon, D Disband, P Pause, F Fog.

Blood & Magic Mechanics

  • Basal Golems & Obelisks: Press Guard (G) or right-click a Basal Golem to turn it into an Obelisk. Obelisks generate Mana over time. Click Transfer (S) to send stored mana to your pool (or let it auto-transfer when full).
  • Summoning: Select your glowing Portal and click Basal Golem (40 mana) (T) to summon a new Basal Golem.
  • Transforming Creatures: Move a Basal Golem next to your Barracks or Crypt, click Transform (T), and pick a creature icon (e.g., Warrior, Ranger, Paladin, Zombie, Gargoyle, Ghoul, Wraith). It forms a Metapod and hatches!
  • Research (XP): Earn XP from combat & building. Click the top-right RESEARCH book or a grayed-out creature icon to unlock higher-tier creatures.
  • 2×2 Foundations: Place 4 Basal Golems in Obelisk mode on all 4 tiles of a stone Foundation, then click Transform (T) to erect a new Arbor Lodge, Barracks, Crypt, Runestone, or Temple!
